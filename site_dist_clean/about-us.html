    ABOUT US

    PURSYS (Zhengzhou Pursys Machinery Co., Ltd.) is a global, export-oriented supplier of powder processing systems. We design and deliver crushing, grinding, classifying, mixing, conveying and dedusting equipment — as well as complete plants — for the chemical, pharmaceutical, food, new-energy, new-material and pigment & ceramic industries.

    About PURSYS — Precision Powder Processing Systems Partner

    Precision in Every Particle

    Powder processing systems you can trust, from single machines to complete production solutions.

    PURSYS is a professional powder processing equipment manufacturer and system solution provider specializing in crushing, grinding, mixing, screening, conveying and packaging technologies.

    We help global industries transform raw materials into precise, stable and reliable powder products through engineering expertise and manufacturing experience.

    Our Story — Understanding Every Particle

    Powder plays an essential role in modern industries. It exists in food ingredients, pharmaceutical products, chemical materials, agricultural products and advanced materials.

    However, producing high-quality powder is not simply about crushing materials.

    Particle size, uniformity, purity and flowability directly influence the final product quality.

    PURSYS was created with one purpose:

    To make every particle more precise.

    We do not only manufacture machines. We design powder processing solutions that help customers achieve stable production, consistent quality and long-term reliability.
